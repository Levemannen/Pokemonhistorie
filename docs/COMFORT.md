# Reading, collecting and coming back

## Calm reading

Reading mode hides the companion sidebar and large page heading while keeping the story, illustration, choices and tools available. The reading toolbar stays within reach as you scroll. Comfortable, Large and Extra large text settings also help with old books, word examples and journey memories. These settings are remembered on the same browser and device.

## A bookshelf of whole adventures

Completing a journey keeps a separate finished book, including the starter, decisions, companions, clues, map, journal, ending and personal after-stories. Starting again replaces only the active journey. Opening an old book never resumes it or changes the active story.

Repeating the same route is a new book, but simply reloading a completed ending does not duplicate it. Earlier ending badges remain in their collection. Only adventures with an available saved journey can become full books; old badges cannot reconstruct journeys that were not stored.

## Adventure bag

The bag shows tools, clues and keepsakes earned during the journey. Each item explains its source and, where applicable, what it was used for. It does not reveal items that have not been found, and it does not claim every keepsake unlocks a route. Finished books preserve their own bags.

## Chapter bookmarks

The four chapter boundaries offer a short recap of actual choices and the team's goal or promise. Continue immediately, ask for narration, or choose Pause here. Reloading preserves a pending chapter bookmark. Continuing a chapter adds no invented story choice and does not reset puzzles or companions. Existing saves resume without an unexpected new pause.

## My Words

Opening an underlined word in the active story collects its meaning with a real sentence from that page, the page title and the starter. Repeated taps and plural forms do not create duplicate entries. The word notebook stays when a new journey starts. Hear a word slowly, read its example aloud, or use the full dictionary.

## Local storage

Progress and collections stay in this browser at this site address. There is no account, cloud sync, backend or paid service. Clearing site storage removes these local records. If the browser cannot save a collection, the app shows an actual saving notice and offers Retry saving; it does not silently trim books. A completed current journey cannot be replaced through Start again while its bookshelf save is failing.

No illustration assets were generated or replaced for this update. Browser screenshots in the documentation show real test journeys rather than preloaded player achievements.
